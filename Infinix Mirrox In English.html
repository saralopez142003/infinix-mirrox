INFINIX MIRROX IN INGLISH


is a Colombian business project located in Soacha, Cundinamarca, created with the idea of designing and selling customized infinity mirrors. Our project aims to transform traditional mirrors into something more modern, creative, and attractive by using LED lights, glass, and reflective materials that create a visual depth effect, making it look as if the lights continue endlessly. We want to offer a product that is not only decorative but also allows people to give their spaces a unique and personal style.


Our target market is mainly young people, gamers, content creators, and people interested in interior decoration. We focus on customers who enjoy technology, modern designs, and products that make their bedrooms, living rooms, studios, or businesses look more attractive. We also want to reach people who are looking for an original gift or a decorative item that is different from the products commonly found on the market.


Initially, our goal is to introduce our brand in Soacha and nearby areas, and later expand our sales to other cities in Colombia. We believe social media is a useful tool to achieve this because it allows us to showcase our products, reach different audiences, and communicate directly with potential customers. We also want to understand our customers' tastes and needs so we can offer designs that match their preferences and budgets.


To find out how interesting our idea could be to potential customers, we conducted a survey of 30 people. The results showed that the product received a positive response and that many people found the idea of owning an infinity mirror attractive. This information helped us better understand potential customers' preferences and recognize that our business could have opportunities in the home decoration market. It also helped us identify the importance of offering eye-catching designs at affordable prices.


As part of the project, we analyzed the materials needed to make the mirrors, production costs, possible selling prices, and strategies to promote the brand. We plan to use platforms such as TikTok and Instagram to publish videos showing the lighting effects, different designs, and manufacturing process. In this way, we hope to attract people's attention, find new customers, and gradually make our brand more recognizable.


Like any new business, we know that we may face some challenges. These include limited initial capital, material costs, and the need to gain more experience in manufacturing and selling our products. For this reason, it is important to manage our resources carefully, calculate expenses, and plan each step to avoid losses and make the business profitable. We must also consider the competition and offer something that makes us stand out, such as customization, creativity, and good customer service.


Our purpose is for INFINIX MIRROX to be more than just an academic idea. We want it to become a real business opportunity. We hope to learn throughout the process, improve our designs, and build a brand that represents innovation and trust. To achieve this, we believe it is essential to listen to customers' opinions, maintain good product quality, and find new ways to promote and sell our mirrors.
