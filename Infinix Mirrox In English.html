Bringing depth, light and innovation to every space.

INFINIX MIRROX is a Colombian business project located in Soacha, Cundinamarca, focused on the creation and sale of customized infinity mirrors.

Our mission is to transform traditional mirrors into modern decorative pieces using LED lighting, reflective materials and innovative designs that create an impressive visual depth effect.

Our target audience includes young people, gamers, content creators, technology enthusiasts and anyone interested in modern interior decoration.

We aim to expand our presence through social media platforms such as Instagram and TikTok, allowing us to showcase our products and connect directly with potential customers.

Market research conducted with potential customers showed a positive response to the concept, encouraging us to continue developing new and innovative designs.

Our goal is to build a trustworthy and creative brand that delivers quality products while offering unique decorative experiences to our customers.
